KeeLoq Lab ESP32-C3 flasher

Plug the board into this computer (Chrome/Edge), pick the unit, press Flash. It writes the merged image at 0x0 (this repartitions to two OTA slots) after clearing the force-download flag (0x600080F4) so the new app boots. If the board is not listed, hold BOOT while plugging it in.
